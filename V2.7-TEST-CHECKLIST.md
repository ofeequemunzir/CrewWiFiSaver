# Crew Connect v2.7 Beta 1 — Onboard Test Checklist

Do the following on a test phone / test account only. Leave the working v2.6 branch available so you can roll back.

1. **No credentials in app:** Open Crew Connect and verify that there are no username or PIN text boxes or in-app sign-in pages.
2. **Sign-in:** While paid internet is offline, tap the circular Wi-Fi button. Confirm that your phone's normal browser opens `https://onboardicafe.com/` (check the address bar), then sign in and select your existing package. Return to Crew Connect using Android Back/Recents if the browser does not auto-close.
3. **Active status:** The app should probe ship Wi-Fi after returning. It should display Connected only when general internet access over ship Wi-Fi is reachable. With cellular data disabled, verify the reading. It cannot read the portal's minute balance from a separate browser.
4. **Instant disconnect:** While paid internet is connected, tap the Wi-Fi circle. Confirm that the paid session becomes restricted before trusting its state; the button should return to offline. No logout website should be visibly opened.
5. **Automatic screen-lock logout:** Enable Auto logout when phone is locked, connect paid internet, then lock the phone. Check actual paid access after unlocking. Repeat while already offline; it should not generate a successful-logout alert just because the screen was locked.
6. **Timer picker:** Enable Auto logout timer. Scroll the hours, minutes and seconds wheels independently (e.g., 00:01:30). The picker disappears when the toggle is off. Confirm that only one logout is requested when the elapsed duration reaches the setting, even if the screen is on.
7. **Free WhatsApp Text:** After logout, the app may show the existing visible automated portal. Verify with a real WhatsApp text. The final portal page alone does not prove activation, and automatic completion while screen-locked is not yet guaranteed.
8. **Failure cases:** Disconnect from ship Wi-Fi, enable mobile data, and confirm Crew Connect does not mistake cellular access for onboard paid internet. Check an unavailable Google probe and ensure it does not falsely announce logout success.

**Note:** Android exact-alarm restrictions, power saving and manufacturer battery management can delay timers. The monitoring service may display a low-priority persistent system notification while automation is enabled. The Google `204` test is a connectivity signal, not a certified paid-account balance or entitlement check.
